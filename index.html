Research · Master's thesisITU Lahore

ClinHallu: Measuring Source-to-Benchmark Shift and the Over-Flagging Bias in Medical Hallucination Detection

Umair Ali, Mamoona Tasadduq (ITU Lahore), Javed Ali Khan (University of Hertfordshire)

Problem

Medical hallucination detectors are usually trained and tested on data from one generation pipeline. Their scores can hide how they behave on a benchmark built a different way, which is the situation every deployed detector faces.

What I built

A controlled five-condition protocol (C1–C5) that trains on 10,000 PubMedQA-derived examples and adds MedHallu examples in steps of 1,000, with validation, test set, calibration and seeds held fixed. Inside it runs GAER++, my parameter-efficient detector: a frozen DeBERTa-v3 encoder with LoRA, answer-to-context cross-attention, token grounding and explicit answer–evidence disagreement features.

Results · 3 seeds, frozen 1,000-example test set
93.75F1, adapted detector (C5)
+35.91MCC points gained from target data
79.7%of the F1 gain from the first 1,000 examples
11.0Mtrainable parameters vs 70B judges

Four zero-shot LLM judges and SelfCheckGPT over-flag correct answers. At 5% hallucination prevalence, GAER++ keeps 50.3% precision against 11.0–27.2% for the judges.

AAXIS AI RAG Assistant

A public company chatbot that answers from live PDFs and shows its sources.

Problem

Clients, job seekers and staff ask the same questions about services, pricing, policies and HR rules. The answers sit across several PDFs that keep changing, and a fine-tuned model would need retraining after every edit.

What it does

New or changed PDFs are picked up by an n8n workflow or folder watcher and become answerable within seconds. Content-hashed chunks mean only changed text is re-embedded and removed text is never cited. Answers come in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, with a Sources panel that opens the PDF at the right page.

Architecture
  1. Detectn8n workflow (Google Drive or local folder) or the built-in watcher calls POST /ingest.
  2. ExtractPyMuPDF text; tables rebuilt as rows, running headers and page numbers removed.
  3. Chunk and diffLangChain chunks with content-hash IDs; only new chunks are embedded, stale ones deleted.

  7. Trace and evaluateLangSmith traces every step; evaluation suite enforces quality gates.

AAXIS InvoiceFlow

Invoice capture with human review, posting bills to QuickBooks Online.

Problem

Accounts payable teams re-key vendor invoices by hand, and duplicate or mis-added invoices slip through and get paid.

What it does

Reads PDFs, scans, phone photos and forwarded emails, checks the math line by line, catches duplicates and unknown vendors, and shows a reviewer exactly where each problem is on the page. Nothing reaches the books without approval, large invoices need two different approvers, and every action lands in a hash-chained audit trail.

Medical Coding AI

Pre-bill coding and claim-integrity assistant for certified coders.

Problem

Coding clinical notes to ICD-10-CM, CPT and HCPCS takes coder time, and unsupported or invalid codes lead to claim denials. An LLM alone can invent codes the note does not support.

What it does

Turns a clinical note into evidence-backed codes with Official Guideline citations. A deterministic rule engine validates every suggestion, the platform computes calibrated confidence instead of trusting the model, and only a human coder can approve. A pre-bill scrubber checks the draft claim before export.
